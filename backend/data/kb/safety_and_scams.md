# How to recognise the official websites
Official PAN services are only on these domains: incometax.gov.in (and eportal.incometax.gov.in), proteantech.in (onlineservices.proteantech.in, tinpan.proteantech.in, tin.tin.proteantech.in) and utiitsl.com (pan.utiitsl.com). Check the address bar before typing anything. Search ads and look-alike domains often charge far more than the official fee shown on the government portal's payment page, or steal Aadhaar and bank details.

# Never share these
- OTPs (Aadhaar OTP, e-Sign OTP, bank OTP) — no genuine official will ask for them on a call or chat.
- Your full Aadhaar number with unknown websites or WhatsApp contacts.
- Card details or UPI PIN anywhere other than the portal's own payment page.

# Paying fees
Pay only on the payment page reached from inside the official portal (UPI, net banking, card or demand draft). The Income Tax portal uses e-Pay Tax for the PAN–Aadhaar late fee. CivicRoute never collects payments, QR codes or OTPs.

# Holding two PANs
Holding more than one PAN is not allowed and can attract a penalty of up to ₹10,000. If you were allotted two PANs, surrender the extra one through the change/correction application or by writing to your Jurisdictional Assessing Officer.

# If your PAN card was stolen
Download an e-PAN or order a reprint. If you suspect misuse, check your Form 26AS / Annual Information Statement on the Income Tax portal for unknown transactions and consider filing a police complaint.
