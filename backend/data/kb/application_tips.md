# Name and date of birth must match your proof
The most common reason PAN applications are rejected is a mismatch between the form and the proof document. Write your name exactly as it appears on your proof (same spelling, same order, no titles like Mr/Shri/Smt). Split it into Last name (surname), First name and Middle name on the form. The date of birth must match the DOB proof exactly. Father's name is mandatory for individuals, even for married women; mother's name is optional unless the father's name is not applicable (for example single mothers can opt to print mother's name).

# Photo and signature specifications (scan-upload mode)
These are the commonly published specifications for scanned uploads on the Protean portal; the portal shows the current limits on the upload screen.
- Photograph: recent colour photo, 3.5 cm × 2.5 cm, 200 dpi, JPEG, maximum 50 KB.
- Signature: 2 cm × 4.5 cm, 200 dpi, JPEG, maximum 50 KB. Sign with black ink on white paper and crop tightly.
In paperless e-KYC mode the photo is taken from Aadhaar and no upload is needed.

# Submission modes
- Paperless (Aadhaar e-KYC + e-Sign): cheapest and fastest, no documents to post; details come from Aadhaar.
- Scanned upload + e-Sign: upload proofs, photo and signature, then e-Sign with Aadhaar OTP.
- Physical: print and sign the acknowledgement, attach photographs and documents, and post them to the address on the acknowledgement within 15 days of applying.

# Correction form: tick only what changes
On the change/correction form every field has a checkbox on its left. Fill all fields, but tick only the ones you want to change, and attach proof for each ticked field (for example a marriage certificate or gazette notification for a name change, or a birth/matriculation certificate for a DOB change).

# Minors and turning 18
A minor's PAN is applied for by a parent or guardian acting as representative assessee; the minor's photo and signature are not needed. When the child turns 18, apply for change/correction to add their own photo and signature.

# NRIs and foreign citizens
NRIs who are Indian citizens use the Indian-citizen application; foreign citizens use the foreign-citizen form. Dispatch outside India costs more; the exact fee is shown on the official payment page (a reprint dispatched abroad is ₹959 per Protean's reprint page).
