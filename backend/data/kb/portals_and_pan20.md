# Three official places for PAN services
PAN is issued by the Income Tax Department (ITD). Applications are processed through three official channels:
- Income Tax e-filing portal (incometax.gov.in / eportal.incometax.gov.in): Instant e-PAN using Aadhaar (free), PAN–Aadhaar linking, link status, Verify Your PAN, Know Your AO.
- Protean eGov Technologies (formerly NSDL e-Gov) at onlineservices.proteantech.in and tinpan.proteantech.in: new PAN, change/correction, reprint, e-PAN download, status tracking.
- UTIITSL at pan.utiitsl.com: the same set of form-based PAN services as Protean.
Protean and UTIITSL are both official and charge the same government fee. Pick one and finish the whole application there. To reprint a card or download an e-PAN it is easiest to use the agency that issued your PAN (or processed your last update).

# Which one should I choose?
- No PAN yet, adult Indian resident, Aadhaar linked to mobile: Instant e-PAN on the Income Tax portal. It is free and paperless.
- Minor, NRI, foreign citizen, company, firm, trust or HUF: form-based application on Protean or UTIITSL.
- Lost card, details unchanged: Reprint on Protean/UTIITSL, or download e-PAN for free/nominal fee.
- Details wrong or changed: Change/Correction on Protean/UTIITSL. If Aadhaar already has the correct details, the Instant e-PAN service on the Income Tax portal also offers updating PAN details as per Aadhaar e-KYC.
- PAN inoperative: link PAN with Aadhaar on the Income Tax portal after paying the late fee.

# PAN 2.0 and the Income-tax Act 2025
The Government approved the PAN 2.0 project on 25 November 2024 to bring all PAN/TAN services onto one unified Income Tax Department platform, with QR-code-enabled PAN cards and free e-PAN. Rollout is happening in phases; until it is complete, the existing portals keep working. Your existing PAN number stays valid — PAN 2.0 does not change your number.
The Income-tax Act 2025 is in force from 1 April 2026. Some PAN application form numbers (earlier Form 49A for Indian citizens and Form 49AA for foreign citizens) may be renamed under the new law. Always follow the form the official portal shows you. PANs issued earlier remain valid.

# What is e-PAN?
e-PAN is a digitally signed PAN card in PDF form. It is a valid proof of PAN everywhere a physical card is accepted. The e-PAN PDF is usually protected with a password equal to your date of birth in DDMMYYYY format.
